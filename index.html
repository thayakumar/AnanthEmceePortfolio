01 — the voice

Still early days, and honestly?
that's the fun part.

I'm Ananth — a Kuala Lumpur based emcee just at the start of the road. A dozen or so events in, mostly around Universiti Malaya, community bodies and government programmes. Not a veteran. Not pretending to be one.

What I bring is energy the room can feel, three languages I can jump between without an accent tripping me up, and a slightly-comedian instinct that keeps things warm even when the run-sheet gets serious.
